# Dropzone 0.1.4

This alpha fixes drag-out completion and makes a following shelf easier to grab.

- Successfully transferred items now leave the shelf automatically.
- Cancelled or rejected drags keep their items.
- The shelf stops early when the pointer moves quickly toward it and locks immediately on mouse-down.

The build targets Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or newer. It is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
